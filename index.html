How it works

Every letter has been swapped for a number from 1 to 26, and the same number is always the same letter. Work out which letter each number stands for to fill the grid with real words.

Colors

  • 9E Given letter. Revealed at the start or by Reveal letter. It can’t be changed.
  • 4S Your letter. Change or clear it any time.
  • 12 Selected square. What you type goes here.
  • 12 Same number. These squares all get the letter you type.
  • 7T Right. Shown for a few seconds after you press Check.
  • 3K Wrong. Crossed out for a few seconds after you press Check.
  • Block. Not part of any word.

The letter key under the grid lists A–Z. Letters you’ve placed show their number underneath, and given letters are shaded gold.

Buttons

Start / Pause
Runs the timer. It’s optional.
Undo
Steps back one change.
Reset
Clears your letters back to the given ones and resets the timer.
Check
Marks every letter you’ve placed as right or wrong.
Reveal letter
Fills in one number you haven’t solved yet.
Reveal all
Shows the whole solution and ends today’s puzzle.

Typing

  • Tap or click a square, then type a letter.
  • Arrow keys move around. Backspace clears. Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on Mac) undoes.
  • Your progress saves in this browser. A new puzzle arrives every day.
